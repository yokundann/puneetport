About

Second-year mechanical engineering student, headed for aerospace.

I study at Madan Mohan Malaviya University of Technology, Gorakhpur. My work so far covers a fixed-wing aircraft I designed, built and flew, introductory CFD in SimScale, and CAD and simulation exercises in SolidWorks and OpenRocket. Outside the syllabus, liquid rocket engines are the rabbit hole I keep going down.

I'm looking for internships and research opportunities in propulsion, aerodynamics and simulation.
